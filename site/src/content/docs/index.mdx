## Pokes, with you in control

Send a friend a zap, buzz or beep and it arrives on their wrist wherever they
are. Every friend can only send what you allowed, each stimulus has its own
intensity cap and cooldown per friend, and a Do Not Disturb switch mutes every
incoming poke. Friends
can even poke you from scripts with API tokens, but only for the stimuli you
allow automated pokes for.

1. **A friend taps poke.** They pick a stimulus you allowed and send it.
2. **The server pushes it.** A notification lands on your phone right away.
3. **Your device fires.** Jolt relays the stimulus to your wearable over Bluetooth.

Pokes go through a [Jolt Server](https://github.com/petrleocompel/jolt-server).
The app lets you point it at any instance under **Settings → Server**.

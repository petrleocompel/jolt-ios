Jolt is an independent companion app for Pavlok wearable devices (Pavlok 2, Pavlok 3, and Shock Clock Max). Not affiliated with or endorsed by Pavlok Inc.

Pair your device over Bluetooth and take control:
- Fire zap, vibration, or beep stimuli with adjustable intensity
- Read live device status: battery, firmware, and connection
- Configure what the physical button does
- Set alarms that live on the device itself, so they still fire even if your phone is off or out of range
- Add a wake-up guarantee — solve a puzzle, do jumping jacks, or scan a QR code across the room before an alarm will stop

Trade jolts with friends:
- Add friends and set exactly what each of them may send you
- Cap zap intensity and set a cooldown, so you stay in control
- Pokes arrive as a push notification and fire on your wrist wherever you are
- A Do Not Disturb switch mutes every incoming poke

Controlling your own device needs no account and works fully offline. The optional friends server is open and can be self-hosted.

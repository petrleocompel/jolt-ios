Jolt is an independent companion app for Pavlok wearable devices (Pavlok 2, Pavlok 3, and Shock Clock Max). Not affiliated with or endorsed by Pavlok Inc.

Pair your device over Bluetooth and:
- Fire zap, vibration, or beep stimuli with adjustable intensity
- Read device status, battery, and firmware information
- Configure what the physical button does
- Set alarms that live on the device itself, so they still fire if your phone is off
- Add a wake-up guarantee — solve a puzzle, do jumping jacks, or scan a QR code before an alarm will stop
